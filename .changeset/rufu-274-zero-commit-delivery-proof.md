summary: Cards are no longer finalized `done` when their branch delivered nothing while the worktree still holds the work.
category: fix
dev: Every lane that can reach the complete lane now proves delivery first. A zero-commit finalization must name where the content landed, otherwise the merge refuses and stamps a durable row hold (`mergeDetails.uncommittedWorkHold` with the at-risk paths, the `paused: true` + `manual-hold` pair, and a `DELIVERY_UNPROVEN:` sentence in `error`), revokes the card's own `mergeConfirmed` claim so the finalizer fast path cannot re-arm it, files a `manual-required` merge work item, and leaves `status`, `mergeRetries`, and the `branch`/`worktree`/`modifiedFiles` delivery pointers untouched so the surviving content can still be committed, discarded, or force-landed. The required pre-merge gates for that decision come from the canonical workflow resolver (shared `resolveNoOpFinalizeGateIds`), so a card with no workflow selection of its own is no longer a dead branch, and post-landing worktree cleanup refuses a held card. New audit surfaces: `task:zero-commit-landing-proof-refused`, `-deferred`, `-cleared`. The guard's required-gate arm asks `evaluatePreMergeApprovals` — the same authority the merge door asks — so a gate the operator waived with an audited bypass (`skipped` + `bypassedBy`) finalizes here instead of being rebound as unfinished work, and the unproven-delivery payload now rides on every blocked reason, so a card blocked both by a gate and by uncommitted content still earns the durable hold instead of losing it to whichever reason won the message.
